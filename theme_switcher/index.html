The Hedgehog's Quest

Spiky ventured onto the ancient bridge, his tiny paws echoing softly.
The milk awaited on the other side—his prize, his dream.

Halfway across, the Hog-Gobbler emerged from the shadows, a grotesque creature of matted fur and hunger.

Its claws scraped the stone. "Your spines won't save you," it snarled.

Spiky didn't run. Instead, he curled tight into a gleaming ball of spikes and rolled directly at the beast. The Hog-Gobbler yelped, leaping aside in pain as spines pierced its paws. Dazed and defeated, the creature retreated into the darkness.

Spiky uncurled and scurried onward. Soon he reached the cup of milk, lapping it gratefully. He'd crossed the bridge, defeated his foe, and won his victory.

A hedgehog's courage knows no bounds.
